The Education Investment Loss

Finland spends on each emigrant's education:

  • Comprehensive school: €80,000
  • Upper secondary: €25,000
  • University degree: €100,000
  • Total per emigrant: ~€150,000-250,000

6,000 net emigrants × €200,000 = €1.2 billion/year in exported education

💥 The Policy Debate

Alarmist view:

"We're losing our best people. Lower taxes for high earners. Create stock option schemes. Match foreign salaries for key talent. This is an emergency."

Relaxed view:

"Brain circulation is normal. Many return with experience. We're gaining immigrants. The country isn't emptying. Don't give tax breaks to the rich."

The hidden story: While 14,500 Finns leave, 45,000 foreigners arrive. Net migration is strongly positive. The question is whether a doctor from India replaces a software engineer moving to California - probably not directly.
