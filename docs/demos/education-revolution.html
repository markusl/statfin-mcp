The Flip

1970: Men had 2x more degrees than women. 2024: Women have 1.5x more degrees than men

The gender education gap reversed completely in 50 years. But nobody's calling it a crisis - yet.

⚡ Why Boys Are Falling Behind

School System

Sitting still, reading, verbal skills - schools reward stereotypically feminine behaviors. Boys are 3x more likely to be diagnosed with learning disorders.

Role Models

80% of teachers are women. Boys see education as "for girls." Few male mentors in schools. Father absence rising.

Alternatives

Vocational paths still lead to good jobs for men (construction, tech). University isn't the only path - but earnings gap is growing.

The Young Generation: Even Larger Gap

Among 25-34 year olds (2024):

  • Women: 58% have tertiary education
  • Men: 35% have tertiary education
  • Gap: 23 percentage points

Young women are almost twice as likely to have a degree as young men.

Where Are The Men?

Male-dominated fields:

  • IT & Computing: 80% men
  • Engineering: 75% men
  • Construction: 90% men
  • Forestry: 85% men

Female-dominated fields:

  • Healthcare: 85% women
  • Education: 80% women
  • Social work: 90% women
  • Psychology: 85% women

💥 The Uncomfortable Questions

Concern view:

"When women lagged, we called it discrimination and fixed it. Now men lag by MORE - and we call it 'choice.' Young men are lost. We need male teachers, boy-friendly pedagogy."

Counter-argument:

"Women STILL earn less. Men still dominate executive positions. A few decades of female majority in education isn't 'crisis' - it's catching up."

The policy vacuum: Finland has no programs specifically targeting boys' educational underperformance. Every gender-in-education initiative focuses on getting women into STEM - not men into anything.
